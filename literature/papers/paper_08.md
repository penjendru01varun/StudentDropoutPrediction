7. **Research Objective:** Student dropout in higher education represents a significant challenge for institutions, learners, and economies alike, resulting in substantial losses of human capital and financial resources. This study presents a machine learning-based early warning system designed to identify students at risk of dropping out following the completion of their first academic semester. Using the Higher Education Predictors of Student Retention dataset, comprising 4,424 student records, a binary classification framework was developed and evaluated across six model configurations-Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM, each in default and class-balanced variants-trained under three resampling conditions: original, oversampled, and undersampled data.
